Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026
Operated by SSQUARE AI SOLUTIONS PVT LTD (“we”, “us”, “our”), Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, India.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Velai mobile application — a hyperlocal job marketplace connecting employers and workers. It does not replace policies for other SSquare products (such as Sense AI) unless we say so.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Velai, we may collect:

  • Account details: mobile number (for OTP login), name, role (employer or worker), profile photo, skills/categories.
  • Location: approximate or precise location to show nearby jobs and workers. If you enable “available for work,” we may collect location while the app is in use or in the background so nearby employers can find you.
  • Job activity: job posts, descriptions, pay you set, accepts/declines, status updates, ratings, and related timestamps.
  • Payments: for the Velai platform posting fee (after free posts), payment is processed by Razorpay. We receive payment status and order references. Worker wages are arranged directly between users (for example cash or UPI) and are not processed as payroll by Velai in the current version.
  • Device & notifications: device identifiers needed for app operation and Expo/Firebase push notification tokens.
  • Support communications: messages you send us by email, phone, or WhatsApp.

2. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account (phone OTP via Firebase Authentication).
  • Match employers and workers near each other and operate job flows.
  • Process platform posting fees and prevent fraud/abuse.
  • Send transactional notifications (new job, acceptance, completion reminders).
  • Improve safety, reliability, and customer support.
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce our Terms.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Other users as needed for a job (for example name, rating, and—after a match/accept—contact details to coordinate work).
  • Service providers: Google Firebase (auth, database, storage, functions), Razorpay (platform fee payments), and Expo (push delivery).
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the platform.

4. Data retention

We keep account and job records for as long as needed to provide Velai, resolve disputes, meet legal/accounting requirements, and improve safety.

You can delete your account directly in the app (Profile → Delete Account). When you do, we immediately cancel any active job posts or acceptances tied to your account and remove your profile, job history, and other personal data from active use. Financial ledger records (such as payment order references) are retained in de-identified form for accounting and audit purposes.

In accordance with India's Information Technology Rules, 2021, we retain a limited archival copy of your account information (such as name and phone number) for 180 days after deletion, so that law enforcement can investigate reports of misuse made against an account before it was deleted. This archive is not accessible through the app and is not used for any other purpose. It is automatically and permanently deleted after 180 days, unless we are legally required to retain it longer because the account is under active investigation.

5. Security

We use industry-standard cloud security controls provided by our processors (including encryption in transit). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please protect your device and do not share OTPs with others.

6. Children’s privacy

Velai is intended for adults who can lawfully work and hire. It is not directed at children under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Your choices

  • You can update profile details in the app.
  • You can disable location or notifications in device settings (some features may stop working).
  • You can delete your account at any time directly in the app (Profile → Delete Account) — see Section 4 for what this does and what is retained.
  • You can also request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by emailing us, if you are unable to use the in-app option.

8. International processing

Our infrastructure providers may process data in India and other regions where their data centres operate. By using Velai, you understand that your information may be processed in those locations under this Policy.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of Velai after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
