VoicePay

Offline UPI payment announcer for shops and merchants

VP
VoicePay

Hear every UPI payment. Loud and clear.

VoicePay listens to credit notifications from PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, and Amazon Pay, then announces the amount (and payer) on your phone speaker — so busy shopkeepers never miss a payment. Works offline on-device. No UPI PIN, no bank login, no cloud sync of your sales.

  • Realtime announce for UPI credits on Android
  • Supports PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay
  • Local history, daily totals, search & CSV export
  • Tamil, Hindi, English and more announcement languages
  • 5-day free trial, then ₹99 one-time for 1 year
  • Payment data stays on your phone (SQLite)
  • Google Play listing in progress
Coming soon on Google Play Privacy Terms
