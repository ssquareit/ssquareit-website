Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026
Operated by SSQUARE AI SOLUTIONS PVT LTD (“we”, “us”, “our”), Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu, India.

This Privacy Policy applies to the VoicePay Android application — an offline UPI payment announcer for merchants and shopkeepers. It does not replace policies for other SSquare products (such as Sense AI or Velai) unless we say so.

1. What VoicePay is (and is not)

VoicePay reads payment credit notifications already shown by UPI apps on your Android phone (for example PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay), extracts amount and payer text when possible, announces them with Text-to-Speech, and stores a local history.

  • VoicePay is not a payment app, UPI PSP, bank, or wallet.
  • VoicePay does not ask for your UPI PIN, bank password, OTP, or card details.
  • VoicePay does not initiate or process UPI transfers.

2. Information we process on your device

Depending on how you use VoicePay, the app may process on your phone:

  • Notification content: title and text of UPI credit notifications (to extract amount/payer and announce).
  • Transaction history: amount, payer name (if available), source app, timestamp, and related notification text — stored in a local SQLite database on your device.
  • App settings: language, volume, repeat count, listening on/off, dark mode, and similar preferences (AsyncStorage / local storage).
  • License status: trial start date and, after purchase, license expiry and Razorpay payment / order identifiers needed to unlock the app.
  • Optional exports: if you use Export, a CSV file is created on your device so you can share it (for example Drive, WhatsApp, email).

3. Permissions

  • Notification access (NotificationListenerService): required to hear UPI payment alerts. Without it, live announcements cannot work.
  • Internet: used for license payment via Razorpay Checkout and (if applicable) app updates from Google Play — not to upload your payment history.

4. Cloud / sharing

VoicePay is designed as offline-first. Your payment announcement history is stored on your device. We do not operate a VoicePay cloud account that syncs your sales ledger in the current version.

  • Razorpay: when you buy the annual license (after the free trial), payment is processed by Razorpay. Razorpay may receive payment details you enter in Checkout. We receive payment status / payment identifiers to activate your license.
  • Google Play: if you install from Play, Google’s own terms and privacy practices apply to distribution, billing alternatives (if any), and crash/analytics they collect.
  • Exports you share: if you export CSV and share it, the recipients you choose will see that data. That sharing is under your control.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Data retention on device

  • History remains on your phone until you clear it (for example after export) or uninstall the app.
  • VoicePay may automatically remove history older than 90 days to keep the local database fresh.
  • License and settings remain until you clear app data or uninstall.

6. Security

Keep your phone locked and Notification Access limited to apps you trust. Because VoicePay can read UPI notification text, treat the device as sensitive business equipment. No method of on-device storage is 100% secure if someone has physical access to an unlocked phone.

7. Children’s privacy

VoicePay is intended for merchants and adults operating a business. It is not directed at children under 18.

8. Your choices

  • Turn Listening off in the app at any time.
  • Revoke Notification Access in Android settings.
  • Export and/or clear local history from the Export screen.
  • Uninstall VoicePay to remove local app data (subject to Android behaviour).

9. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of VoicePay after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
